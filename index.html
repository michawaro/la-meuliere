4 rooms in an authentic millstone house, 2 minutes from the station.

A millstone house in Bures-sur-Yvette, set in a handsome wooded garden. The RER B is right next to it, and Université Paris-Saclay is accessible on foot or by bicycle depending on the buildings. This is a house, not a residence.
